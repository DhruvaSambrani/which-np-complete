THE PRAGMATIST

Class: BQP (Bounded-error Quantum Polynomial time)

You are the comfortable probabilist. BQP represents the set of problems that a realistic quantum computer can solve efficiently while accepting a small, bounded margin of error.

You understand that life is noisy, chaotic, and uncertain. You don't need 100% perfection; you just want to be right most of the time. You process complex situations rapidly by trusting your intuition and accepting that occasionally being wrong is just the cost of doing business efficiently. You don't panic when things go slightly off-script—you just adapt the odds in your favor.
