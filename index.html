THE OVERTHINKER

Your brain is a supercomputer that naturally sees the domino effect of every choice. You are prone to analysis paralysis because you know that choosing A means giving up B, which forces C to happen. You are the ultimate truth-seeker, loophole-finder, and rule-follower.
